<h2>&#10024; Overview</h2>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="65%">
<p>Majestic is a magic and adventure mod built around the sky itself: stars, constellations, light and ether. Power isn't handed to you &mdash; you find it in the world, refine it through ritual, and carry it as a spell, an artifact, or knowledge no one else has. Three linked layers &mdash; <strong>rituals &amp; research</strong>, <strong>spells</strong>, and <strong>relics</strong> &mdash; each unlock the next, gated behind exploration.</p>

<p>Majestic is currently in <strong>early alpha</strong>: one school, four spells, a focus, and the essence resource that powers them. Rituals, the research tree, relics, a dedicated dimension, structures and bosses are on the roadmap &mdash; see the version history for what's implemented today.</p>

<h2>&#127919; Features (Alpha)</h2>

<h3>&#127775; The Starlight School</h3>
<p>Four spells cast through a single focus: <strong>Starlight Bolt</strong> (a piercing hitscan that damages and blinds), <strong>Starlight Ward</strong> (Absorption and Regeneration for yourself), <strong>Starlight Reveal</strong> (marks nearby hostiles with Glowing), and <strong>Starlight Surge</strong> (a burst of Speed).</p>

<h3>&#128302; Essence</h3>
<p>A single magic resource that regenerates passively when you're out of combat. A HUD readout appears whenever you hold your focus, showing your current essence and its cap.</p>

<h3>&#128302; The Starlight Focus</h3>
<p>Your channel for casting. Right-click to cast; each spell respects its own cooldown. The ritual to change which spell your focus casts is still on the roadmap &mdash; for now it always casts Starlight Bolt.</p>

<br>

<h2>&#128302; What's Next</h2>

<p>Rituals and altars, a research tree of constellations, relics with set synergies, a dedicated Celestial Plane dimension with its own structures and bosses, a full questline, and an in-game guide book. Follow the version history for progress.</p>

<h2>&#127918; How to Use</h2>

<ol>
<li>Install NeoForge 21.1.249 for Minecraft 1.21.1.</li>
<li>Install Majestic together with <a href="https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/astral-core">Astral Core</a> and <a href="https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/almanac-core">Almanac Core</a>, on both client and server.</li>
<li>There's no crafting recipe for the focus yet &mdash; grab it from the creative inventory or with <code>/give @s majestic:starlight_focus</code>.</li>
<li>Run <code>/majestic status</code> in-game to check your essence and spell cooldowns.</li>
</ol>
