<h2>v0.0.0-beta.3 - Astral Altar, research and the Almanac guide book</h2>

<p>The first real ritual and progression loop: build an altar, research the first light, and engrave a spell onto your focus.</p>

<h3>Added</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Astral Altar (Tier 1)</strong>: a small multiblock (altar + 4 astral pillars) that runs the <code>engrave_spell</code> ritual.</li>
<li>Hold your focus in your main hand and a spell sigil in your off-hand, interact with the altar &#8212; consumes a blank page and essence, engraves that spell onto your focus. Requires the <code>first_light</code> research node.</li>
<li><strong>Research</strong>: the <code>first_light</code> node, auto-unlocked the first time you cast with your focus.</li>
<li><strong>Guide book</strong> (<em>The Almanac</em>, via Vellumli if installed): documents the 4 spells, the engrave ritual, and first_light. Delivered to you, with its advancement, when first_light unlocks.</li>
<li>New items: blank page and 4 spell sigils (bolt / ward / reveal / surge).</li>
<li>The focus's cast spell is no longer fixed &#8212; it remembers whichever spell you last engraved onto it.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Fixed</h3>
<ul>
<li>A datagen path bug meant generated spell data was never actually read by Almanac Core &#8212; fixed (the hand-written alpha content already lived at the correct path, so this had no visible effect until now).</li>
</ul>

<blockquote><strong>Early alpha.</strong> Tier 2 of the altar (and the Observatory structure it belongs to) isn't built yet &#8212; that's the next milestone. Ritual reagents are checked from your inventory, not from the pillars themselves.</blockquote>
