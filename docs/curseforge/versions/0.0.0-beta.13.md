<h2>v0.0.0-beta.13 - Warden of the Gate, combat rework</h2>

<p>The Act II boss now fights with a real repertoire instead of a single forward chop.</p>

<h3>Changed</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Three attacks chosen by the situation</strong>: a wide horizontal <strong>sweep</strong> that hits everyone in front of it, an overhead <strong>slam</strong> with a shockwave when several players crowd it, and a quick <strong>thrust</strong> against targets just out of reach. Damage now lands on the impact frame of each animation.</li>
<li>Livelier idle and walk; in phase two the Warden <strong>runs</strong>.</li>
<li>New phase-two <strong>charge</strong>: a clearly telegraphed wind-up with a line of light on the ground, then a straight rush. If it slams into a wall it is stunned for a moment &#8212; punish it.</li>
</ul>

<blockquote><strong>Early alpha.</strong> Some progression items (Starlight Focus, Astral Altar, Astral Pillar) still cannot be obtained in survival &#8212; a progression pass is coming. Feedback on the fight is welcome.</blockquote>
