<h2>v0.0.0-beta.16 - The Journey takes shape</h2>

<p>Fixes from the first playtest of the Journey, plus the art for everything added in beta.14.</p>

<h3>Added</h3>
<ul>
<li>Models, animations and textures for the four night creatures (Fallen Watcher, Stargazer Cultist, Meteor Crawler, Umbral Moth) &#8212; they are no longer invisible.</li>
<li>Textures for the Page of the Beginning and the Shrine Page.</li>
<li><strong>"The Arcane Journey"</strong> advancement tab: it opens the first time you join a world, then tracks finding a Fallen Shrine, reaching the Observatory and defeating the Warden of the Gate.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Changed</h3>
<ul>
<li>The Fallen Shrine now has a single chest (one Shrine Page per shrine).</li>
<li>Night creature attacks now hit on the impact frame of their animation.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Fixed</h3>
<ul>
<li>The Astral Altar was invisible when placed.</li>
<li>Journey chapters in the Almanac appeared out of order.</li>
<li>Missing icons in the Journey category of the Almanac.</li>
</ul>

<blockquote><strong>Early alpha.</strong> The Warden's staff grip and the Fallen Shrine / Observatory are being reworked, and the Almanac will get pictures of what to look for. Some progression items (Starlight Focus, Astral Altar, Astral Pillar) still cannot be obtained in survival.</blockquote>
