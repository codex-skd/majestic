<h2>v0.0.0-beta.14 - The Journey begins</h2>

<p>The Almanac becomes a real journey: recovered journal pages reveal, chapter by chapter, where to go next.</p>

<h3>Added</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>The Journey</strong>, a new Almanac category. It opens with a <strong>Prologue</strong> &#8212; a riddle about where to begin &#8212; and two chapters unlocked by pages of the Order of Watchers' journal.</li>
<li><strong>Four night creatures</strong> that roam the Overworld after dark: the <strong>Fallen Watcher</strong> (undead, melee, burns in sunlight), the <strong>Stargazer Cultist</strong> (hurls starlight bolts from afar), the <strong>Meteor Crawler</strong> (climbs walls) and the <strong>Umbral Moth</strong> (swoops from the sky). Each has a spawn egg and its own drops.</li>
<li><strong>Page of the Beginning</strong>: carried by the Fallen Watchers. Read it with the Almanac in your inventory to unlock <em>Chapter I &#8212; The Fallen Shrines</em>.</li>
<li><strong>Shrine Page</strong>: found in the Fallen Shrine's chest. Once Chapter I is read, it unlocks <em>Chapter II &#8212; The Observatory</em>&#8230; and a warning about what guards it.</li>
<li>Full Spanish translation for all of the above.</li>
</ul>

<blockquote><strong>Early alpha.</strong> The four new creatures' models and the journal page textures are still being made: for now the creatures are <strong>invisible</strong> (they still spawn and fight) and the pages show the missing-texture checkerboard. Some progression items (Starlight Focus, Astral Altar, Astral Pillar) still cannot be obtained in survival.</blockquote>
