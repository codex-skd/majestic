<h2>v0.0.0-beta.17 - Landmarks worth finding</h2>

<p>The first two stops of the Journey get a proper look, and the Almanac now shows you what to search for.</p>

<h3>Changed</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Warden of the Gate</strong>: new model that actually grips its staff in a closed fist, and stronger, more readable animations for the sweep, slam, thrust, walk and run.</li>
<li><strong>Fallen Shrine</strong> redesigned as a ruined chapel of the Order: broken arches, an altar niche with the Order's star and a single chest.</li>
<li><strong>Observatory</strong> reworked with a monumental entrance (two tuff towers, the Order's star above the door, a broken instrument on top) and more detail inside.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Added</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Pictures in the Almanac</strong>: Chapter I shows a Fallen Shrine and Chapter II the Observatory gate.</li>
</ul>

<blockquote><strong>Early alpha.</strong> The new structures only appear in newly generated chunks. Some progression items (Starlight Focus, Astral Altar, Astral Pillar) still cannot be obtained in survival.</blockquote>
