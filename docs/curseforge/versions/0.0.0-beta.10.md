<h2>v0.0.0-beta.10 - Boss I: Warden of the Gate</h2>

<p>The Observatory's arena finally has its guardian.</p>

<h3>Added</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Warden of the Gate</strong> (Act II boss). Awakens the first time a player steps into an Observatory's arena &#8212; one per structure. Two phases: it summons Astral Constructs, and below half health it floods the arena with telegraphed pulses of light that hurt and blind anyone in its line of sight. Take cover behind the columns.</li>
<li>Boss bar, health and damage scaled by the number of players in the arena, full reset if everyone leaves.</li>
<li>Every participant gets their own reward: the <strong>Ether Lens</strong> (key to the next act), Astral Dust and Star Fragments.</li>
<li><strong>Astral Construct</strong>, the Warden's minion.</li>
<li>Spawn eggs for both creatures and the <em>Beyond the Gate</em> advancement.</li>
</ul>

<blockquote><strong>Early alpha.</strong> The Astral Construct's model and the Ether Lens texture are still in progress &#8212; the construct is invisible and the lens shows the missing-texture checkerboard for now. The fight has not been fully playtested yet; feedback welcome.</blockquote>
